# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Smart India Hackathon judges and first-time reviewers evaluating BLUEVERSE in a short demonstration. Scientific and technical users also use the workspace to inspect model fields, observations, collocations, and historical-event analysis.

## Product Purpose

BLUEVERSE makes real ocean-model and in-situ datasets explorable in one browser-based 3D/4D scientific workspace. Success means a first-time user can understand where they are, what variable they are viewing, how it is rendered, which analysis is active, and where to inspect results within roughly ten seconds.

## Positioning

The product combines source-aware ocean visualization, QC-aware model-observation comparison, and isolated historical-event analysis without blurring model products, reanalysis, and observations.

## Operating Context

The primary workflow is Where, What, Explore, Analyse, then Inspect. The Cesium ocean globe is the main working surface. Time and exact source depth remain continuously controllable. A contextual inspector reveals scientific results and provenance when requested or when a selection produces a result.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scientific calculations, API semantics, dataset identifiers, units, temporal matching, Argo collocation, historical phase calculations, and U/V vector conventions are locked.
- Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea model workflows, observations, probe, deviation analysis, 3D, isosurface, and transect capabilities remain available only where supported.
- Cyclone Amphan historical mode preserves daily, phase-mean, difference, track-linked inspection, and return-to-current behavior.
- Historical 3D, historical isosurface, and historical transect are unsupported.
- Provenance, unavailable states, precise timestamps, exact source depths, and source units must remain visible.

## Brand Commitments

BLUEVERSE uses a restrained dark scientific workstation identity, cyan for model/scientific state, amber for historical-event state, precise technical typography, and the Cesium globe as its visual focus. It must not resemble a generic SaaS dashboard or consumer application.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains real Copernicus model subsets, Argo profiles, a Spray glider mission, a normalized NOAA IBTrACS Cyclone Amphan track, GLORYS12V1 historical reanalysis, deterministic scientific tests, and source/provenance documentation. No testimonials, customer metrics, or unsupported capability claims are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Scientific truth precedes visual polish.
- The ocean remains the primary visual and interaction surface.
- Progressive disclosure separates immediate results from implementation metadata.
- Every active tool tells the user what to do next.
- Unsupported analysis remains explicit rather than simulated.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, readable contrast, responsive access from 390 px upward, and approximately 44 px touch targets are required.
